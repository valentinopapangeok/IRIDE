# Processing geometry companion report

After thematic reporting, `report/positional_geometry.json` lists each passing product date or exact passing hour and area. For S5-02-01 through S5-02-11, the positional result is an official processing-geometry KPI on traceable source grids/contributors. Product 04 measures source-to-final last-write assignments for verified rainy cells; its gauge/reference support remains separate. Product 05 optional VIIRS replacement occurs on the OLCI grid and does not alter its positional geometry; product 06 uses the mandatory MTG/FCI Cloud Mask geolocation (`cloud_mask` is the ecCodes decoder name for the service's `cloud_state` request); product 07 uses the mandatory Sentinel-5P PAL grid and does not use GOSAT for positional displacement; product 08 uses mandatory OCO2/OCO3 histogram-bin placement and does not use GOSAT for positional displacement; product 09 uses mandatory MODIS MCD19A2.061 native-grid geometry and does not use AERONET for positional displacement; products 10 and 11 use all same-day mandatory Sentinel-3 SL_2_LST/SL_2_WST geolocation points against the final 300 m footprints and do not use Landsat for positional displacement. For 10/11, parent-to-child grid construction is recorded separately and SRTM alignment is diagnostic only: SRTM is a model feature, not the official positional reference. All positional evidence remains separate from thematic KPI decisions and accepted campaign samples.

The report reads optional evidence records from `positional/evidence/` under the configured workdir. A record is keyed by product, UTC date/hour and area, and must carry the matching delivery revision. Missing evidence or a revision mismatch is `UNASSESSED`; it is never interpreted as zero displacement. Permitted statuses are `MEASURED_LINEAGE`, `GRID_ONLY`, `CODE_BOUND`, and `UNASSESSED`. For product 01 the operative quantity is `native_to_final_footprint_distance_m`; centre distance is retained only as a diagnostic. Other geometric quantities must be named explicitly, such as `native_to_final_assigned_centre_m`, `source_grid_to_final_cell_centre_distance_m` or `grid_lattice_residual_m`. Interpolation support is a separate quantity. Product 06's declared tolerance is 3,000 m and its official KPI is the percentage of final cells strictly below that tolerance.

An evidence JSON record must contain `product`, `date`, `area`, `revision`, `status`, `quantity`, `max_m`, and `assessed_cells`; it should also retain input/output checksums, the method version, coverage fraction and any limitations. For hourly products, name the file `S5-02-03_YYYYMMDDHH_North.json` (substitute product, hour and area); daily products use `S5-02-01_YYYYMMDD_North.json`. The post-report stage reuses checksum-receipted inputs or downloads mandatory inputs through the external DCO configuration. Product-specific producer adapters still need exact contributor lineage or an applicable code bound before they can populate final-value displacement across all dates.

The October pilot evidence and limitations are retained in the separate local study `validation_app/positional_proxy_20261113/OCTOBER_ONE_DATE_PILOT.md`. Its local paths and raw inputs are not part of this repository.
