# Precipitation reference-quality review

`precipitation_qc.py` implements a repeatable **review-flag** layer, separate from station-hour acceptance and the operative denominator. A radar–gauge difference is an investigation flag only: radar is also a product input. It never authorizes reference rejection. Product agreement does not authorize keeping a reference that independent evidence establishes invalid.

`review_reference` uses gauge amounts and coordinates only. It inspects complete same-hour references within 10 km, counts nearby potentially colocated sites conservatively once at 100 m separation, and reports insufficient support explicitly. The strict exploratory rule flags >=10 mm at a target with at least three distinct neighbours all below 0.2 mm. A secondary rule flags large discrepancies with at least five relatively homogeneous neighbours. Parameters are explicit in `DEFAULT_POLICY` and are project sensitivity criteria, not a claimed provider or universal QC standard.

Flags require review, not automatic exclusion. Local convection and terrain can create valid spatial differences. Preserve every flag, neighbour value/distance, period, provider and available native-input evidence. A station-hour can be provisionally removed in a clearly labelled **sensitivity analysis**; confirmed-invalid reference exclusions require documented independent evidence and a bounded observation interval. Apply the same criteria to passing and failing observations. Removing an entire station requires evidence of persistent sensor/measurement failure, not a large product error.

Publish original and QC-qualified **passing/evaluable rainy station-hours** by UTC date with North/Center/South columns, excluded counts/reasons and the unchanged 20% acceptance threshold. Dry target observations are not in that denominator, but dry neighbouring observations remain essential evidence for identifying isolated implausible rain. Preserve raw downloads and do not silently update historical counts. Retrospective exclusion sensitivity does not establish independent improvement; freeze selection/QC rules before new evaluation dates.

The module does not alter `PrecipitationAdapter` eligibility or automatically discard any observation. Its offline tests verify independent neighbour logic, corroborating rain, colocated support, missing radar and separation of radar disagreement from reference rejection.
